How it works

Three happy steps from blank page to fridge door.

1

Pick a page

Browse 10,000 unique drawing patterns by category, or hit “Surprise me” and let luck pick for you.

2

Tap to fill

Every shape is a little pot of paint. Tap it with a colour and watch the picture bloom.

3

Save or print

Keep your masterpiece as a picture, print it on A4, or start another one straight away.
